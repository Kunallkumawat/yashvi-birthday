YASHVI BIRTHDAY SURPRISE 💗

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser to preview.
3. To publish: upload index.html and the complete photos folder to the ROOT of your GitHub repository.
4. In GitHub: Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root) > Save.
5. Wait for the Pages URL, then test it on a phone.

Important:
- Keep the photos folder beside index.html. Do not rename it.
- The page is responsive and works on modern Android/iPhone browsers.
- The envelope opens the Hinglish letter. Photos open in a larger view.
- The countdown uses November 22 and automatically rolls to next year after the birthday.
- Background music is not included; phones usually block autoplay unless the visitor taps play.
